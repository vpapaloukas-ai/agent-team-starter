argument-hint: [what we just did]

Run a short retrospective on: **$ARGUMENTS** (or the work just completed).

Separate two kinds of signal:

1. **Product issues** — bugs or gaps in what we built → note them as follow-up tasks.
2. **Process friction** — where the agents, commands, hooks, or `CLAUDE.md` slowed us down or misfired → **this is the valuable part.**

For each process-friction item, capture: what happened · the root cause · a concrete fix (edit an agent, a command, a hook, or `CLAUDE.md`). Append the triaged results to `docs/decisions-log.md` under today's date.

Keep it terse — signal, not ceremony. Apply the obvious fixes now; leave the rest as noted follow-ups.
